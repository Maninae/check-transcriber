        How to paste the photo from Gmail
        1. In Gmail, click the photo attachment so it opens large.
        2. Right-click the photo and choose Copy image.
        3. Come back to this tab and press Ctrl+V.

        How to fix the outlines
        • Missed a check? Click Add a check, then drag a box around it. It snaps to the edges.
        • Not a check? Click its outline, then the red × (or press Delete).
        • A little off? Drag a corner. Amber outlines deserve a second look.
